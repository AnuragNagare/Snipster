SNIPSTER - POSTING SCHEDULE (India + International audience)
============================================================

WHAT IS SOLID AND WHAT ISN'T
- Solid: Shorts and Reels are pushed mainly by the algorithm, not by your
  subscribers' feeds. Retention and swipe-away rate in the first few seconds
  matter far more than the posting hour. Timing is a modest lever.
- Solid: Posting 30-60 minutes before a high-activity window helps. The
  platform needs time to test the clip on a small audience before the peak.
- Not known: The exact best hour for your niche. That depends on who watches
  you. YouTube Studio shows it for your channel once you have some views:
  Analytics -> Audience -> "When your viewers are on YouTube".
- Treat everything below as a hypothesis to test for 3-4 weeks, not a guarantee.


WHY THESE WINDOWS (IST = UTC+5:30)
India's prime time lines up with morning or afternoon in the West.

  IST        US Eastern   US Pacific   UK
  12:30 PM   ~3 AM        ~12 AM       ~8 AM
  8:00 PM    ~10:30 AM    ~7:30 AM     ~3:30 PM
  9:00 PM    ~11:30 AM    ~8:30 AM     ~4:30 PM
  11:30 PM   ~2 PM        ~11 AM       ~7 PM

US figures use daylight time. In winter subtract one hour for the US; for the
UK the gap becomes 5.5 hours.

India has by far the largest audience, so anchor on Indian habits and pick
slots that also catch the West.


STARTING SCHEDULE (2 slots a day)
- Slot A: 12:00-12:30 PM IST
  Indian lunch break; UK is waking up.
- Slot B: 7:30-8:30 PM IST  (main slot)
  Indian evening peak; US waking up, UK in the afternoon.
- Optional late slot: ~11 PM IST
  Indians scroll late; US East coast is mid-afternoon. Test it, don't rely on it.

  Mon-Fri    Slot A and Slot B
  Saturday   One post at 10-11 AM IST and one at 8 PM IST
             (weekend usage is spread out later)
  Sunday     Slot B, plus a second evening post if you have material

This needs roughly 10-14 clips a week. The app makes about 5 clips per long
video, so 2-3 source videos a week would cover it. If that's too much, start
with 1 post a day at Slot B. Consistency matters more than volume.


INSTAGRAM REELS
Use the same evening window (8-10 PM IST); Instagram skews toward evening and
weekend activity. Cross-post the same clip a few hours after YouTube rather
than at the same minute.


HOW TO FIND YOUR REAL BEST TIMES
1. For 3-4 weeks, vary the slots across weekdays and note first-24-hour views
   for each post.
2. Compare by slot, not by single clips - one viral clip will mislead you.
3. Then check the YouTube Studio "when your viewers are on" chart and shift
   toward the hours that show a pattern.
4. If your audience is mostly Indian, the Western overlap matters less. If
   analytics show 30%+ from the US or UK, lean into Slot B and the late slot.


WHAT MOVES VIEWS AND FOLLOWERS MORE THAN TIMING
- A strong hook in the first 1-2 seconds. Check each clip opens on the
  interesting part.
- One clear idea per clip, usually under 45 seconds.
- Captions readable on mute (the renderer already burns them in).
- A consistent niche, so the algorithm knows who to show you to.
- Replying to comments in the first hour.


CAVEAT ABOUT THE SNIPSTER APP
In the code as it stands, the "scheduled publish" option creates a job but
nothing runs it later. To post at these times today, click publish at the
right time or use YouTube Studio's own scheduling.
